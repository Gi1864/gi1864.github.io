I take pride in the fact that I know his preferences, likes, and dislikes very well.

I know he is curious to Learn about different things.

But sometimes he's too much off the track that he forgets where he's heading to. It makes him vulnerable and weak, so weak that he himself starts doubting own abilities.

Whenever he is alone or goes into introspection, I am the one to make decisions for him because he knows I am pure/ honest and always there to suggest the best things which can make him succeed in life. Everything I suggest to him can make his life better and I know exactly what he wants.

He always listens to what I say and pays attention to it as well. Sometimes repenting and regretting that he disobeyed me. But God knows why, after some time, he again disobeys me.

Every time I tell him to do the things which are best for him. He would do the straight opposite of it. Like I made a plan for him to study a list of books which are extremely important for his upcoming examination but he chose to read some random articles which have nothing to do with his examination nor it will help him anyway. He spent the day reading stuff that wasn't relevant at the time.

At times I warn him about the consequence of this and he understands it as well but eventually goes off the track again.

I believe I am the best to advise him. He might need expert advice on some of the things, but right now my advice can get him closer to it.

For any matter, no one can give him better advice than me, even a top adviser of the world. I know his weaknesses and strengths better than anyone else in the world. I am aware of his present and of the past more than anyone else and I also know where he wants to go. This behaviour of him going off the track can't make him a failure because I know he'll do well. It's just a temporary thing but it has certainly delayed achieving what he aimed for. I am not fearful or have low self-esteem but it makes me concerned for him.

I might have all the answers except this.

Why does he get more inclined/ interested to do the things which are less important at the time?

Does he need another person to advise, control or a mentor to monitor every time/ everything he does?
