To be a good leader, emotions and enthusiasm is not just enough, along with this he/she should must have intellectuality.

All the candidates and students are full of enthusiasm during student union election over the past few years. I have seen that all the candidates are making big promises and giving long speeches. And the general students are dreaming for a better facilities and environment in the University level and representation on the national level but when election is over, they forget the promises or is not able to fulfil.

We can say that over the past few years we had enough oratory skilled candidates but after watching their some activities I am in dilemma to say that we had enough intellectual candidates. That’s why our representation in the national level is not satisfactory. There is a big cause of this problem is lobbism and regionalism. We should endeavour to mobilise general student at the University level and general public at national level.

I expect this year we should not only have speech giving union but fulfil their promises and rise out of the campus shining bright in the horizon of India.

**To all the contestants of #AMUSU**

Each of you possesses commendable leadership qualities, vision, public speaking skills, and a desire to effect change within the system. While not everyone can emerge victorious in the election, every participant gains valuable experience and learns something valuable. Originating from different corners of the country, regardless of whether you win or lose, I implore you to continue utilizing your exceptional qualities to amplify the voices of suppressed communities or classes, at least within your own regions. Remember, your objective is not solely to win the election but to contribute towards the betterment of the Nation. I extend my heartfelt congratulations to all the newly elected members of #AMUSU 2k18.

P. S. - We celebrate the AMUSU elections as a festival, and we are all engaged with full interest and enthusiasm. Last year Sajad Subhan Rather loudly said in his final speech, *"Mai Kashmiri nhi, Mai Bihari nhi, Mai Bengali nhi, Mai UP nhi, Mai ALIG hu... Yaad rakho, yaad rakho, tumpe dunya ki nazre hai, tumpe ek kishan jo Gujarat me hai, tumpe ek kishan jo Bihar me hai, tumpe ek kishan jo Kashmir me hai, tumse expectation rakhta hai ki tum uski baat karoge".* This was powerful and this made him victorious also, but it remained only a brilliant and an eloquent speech. Being a student at AMU, everyone has a thought while taking part in it. These are a few words from me.
